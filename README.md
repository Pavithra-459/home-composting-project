🌱 Home Composting Project

About the Project

Home composting is a simple and eco-friendly way to convert kitchen waste and other biodegradable materials into nutrient-rich compost for plants.

Objectives

- Reduce household organic waste.
- Promote recycling and environmental awareness.
- Produce natural compost for plants and gardens.
- Encourage sustainable living practices.

Process

1. Collect biodegradable kitchen waste.
2. Separate organic waste from plastic and other non-biodegradable materials.
3. Place suitable organic waste in a compost container.
4. Allow it to decompose with proper aeration and moisture.
5. Use the finished compost to enrich soil.

Benefits

- Reduces household waste.
- Supports healthy soil.
- Reduces the need for chemical fertilizers.
- Encourages eco-friendly habits.
